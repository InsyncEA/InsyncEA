Customer Success for a WhatsApp-Based AI Product: Supporting Subscription Adoption in Kenya

I supported the introduction and growth of a WhatsApp-based AI product in the Kenyan market, working on customer success, subscription adoption and the practical problems new users hit along the way.

The business challenge

New products meet real-world friction. Customers were unsure how the product fit their needs, and payment problems with M-Pesa and PayPal could stop a subscription before it started.

My approach

  • Identified customer challenges and gathered user feedback to understand needs.
  • Supported customers through subscription onboarding.
  • Troubleshot payment issues involving M-Pesa and PayPal.
  • Researched the market to understand what local users expected.
  • Fed what I learned back into improving the customer experience.

What customer success means for a new product

With a new product, the early customers show you what works and what does not. Customer success means helping each of them get started, solving the problems they hit, and turning what you learn into a better product and a better first experience.

Why payment support drives subscription adoption

A subscription only starts if the payment goes through. When customers are unsure how to pay, or a payment fails, many simply stop. In Kenya, mobile money such as M-Pesa is a common way to pay, so supporting customers through payment issues is part of supporting adoption.

How to support subscription onboarding

  1. Understand what the customer wants before explaining features.
  2. Guide them through sign-up and payment, step by step.
  3. Resolve payment problems quickly, with clear instructions for each method.
  4. Follow up to check that the subscription is active and working.
  5. Record the common questions and share them with the team.

Tools used

  • WhatsApp
  • M-Pesa
  • PayPal

The outcome

Customers had clearer help getting subscribed and resolving payment problems, and the team gained a better view of what users in this market needed.

Signs your product needs more customer support

  • People sign up or enquire but never complete the subscription.
  • Customers keep asking the same questions about payment or setup.
  • You are not sure what users in a new market expect.

Questions about this work

What is customer success for a new product?

It is helping new customers get real value from a product: understanding what they need, supporting them through onboarding, solving the problems they hit and feeding what is learned back to the team.

What did this work cover?

Identifying customer challenges and gathering user feedback, supporting subscription onboarding, troubleshooting M-Pesa and PayPal payment issues, researching what local users expected and feeding what was learned back into the customer experience.

Why do payment problems matter for subscriptions?

A payment problem at the point of subscribing can end the sign-up before it starts. In Kenya, mobile money such as M-Pesa is a common way to pay, so helping customers through payment issues directly supports adoption.

How is user feedback used?

Feedback from customer conversations is recorded as common themes, so the team can see what customers expected and what confused them, and improve the experience.
