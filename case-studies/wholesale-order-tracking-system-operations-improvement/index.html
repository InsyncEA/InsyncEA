Wholesale Order Tracking System: Bringing Orders from Shopify, Portals and Email into One View

A wholesale tea business had order information spread across Shopify, wholesale portals, supplier communication and email. I created a more organized tracking process so the status of every order was easy to see.

The business challenge

Answering a simple question such as where an order was or whether an invoice was paid meant checking several places. That made follow-up slow and manual, and details could easily be missed.

Every extra place an order can live adds a step to every question. When orders arrive from a store, from portals and by email, the real status is rarely in one spot, and it takes someone’s time to piece it together.

My approach

  • Mapped where order information lived: Shopify, wholesale portals, supplier communication and email.
  • Built one organized tracker covering order information, tracking numbers, delivery dates and invoice status.
  • Set a consistent way of updating it, so it stayed current.
  • Used the tracker to support supplier coordination and follow-ups.
  • Refined the layout as the business used it.

What a wholesale order tracking system should include

A good tracker answers the questions you ask every day without opening anything else. A typical layout has:

  • The order: who it is for and what was ordered.
  • The source: where the order came from, such as a store, a portal or email.
  • The status: processing, shipped or delivered.
  • Tracking and delivery dates: so you can answer “where is it?” at a glance.
  • Invoice status: sent, paid or overdue.

How to build a simple order tracker

  1. List every place orders live, including portals and inboxes.
  2. Choose the fields you need to answer your most common questions.
  3. Agree an update routine: who updates it, when, and from which sources.
  4. Use it for follow-ups, including supplier coordination and overdue invoices.
  5. Review and refine the layout as the business uses it.

Tools used

  • Shopify
  • Wholesale portals
  • Spreadsheets

The outcome

The business gained better visibility into orders and needed far less manual follow-up, because the key details were in one place.

Signs you need an order tracking system

  • Simple questions about an order mean checking several places.
  • Invoices or deliveries slip because nobody has the full picture.
  • Follow-up with suppliers depends on someone remembering.

Questions about wholesale order tracking

What is a wholesale order tracking system?

It is one organized place to see every wholesale order: where it came from, its status, tracking number, delivery date and invoice status, so nobody has to check several systems to answer a simple question.

What did the tracker cover in this case?

Order information, tracking numbers, delivery dates and invoice status, brought together from Shopify, wholesale portals, supplier communication and email.

Do I need special software for order tracking?

Not necessarily. In this case the tracker was built in a spreadsheet, using tools the business already had.

How do you keep a tracker up to date?

A consistent way of updating it is what keeps it current: agree who updates it, when, and from which sources, then review it regularly.
