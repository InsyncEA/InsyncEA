Protecting a Nonprofit Founder's Time with Organized Calendar Management

A nonprofit founder had a calendar full of interviews, partnership meetings, press opportunities, and sponsorship discussions. I supported the founder as executive assistant, taking over calendar coordination and the follow-ups that come with it.

The business challenge

Every request competed for the same limited time. Without a clear way to weigh them, double bookings, missed follow-ups, and high-value conversations being squeezed out by routine ones were constant risks.

My approach

  • Coordinated the calendar across interviews, partnership meetings, press opportunities, and sponsorship discussions.
  • Scheduled around the founder's priorities, so the most important engagements got protected time.
  • Handled meeting coordination: invitations, timing, and the details each meeting needed.
  • Tracked follow-ups after each conversation, so agreed next steps did not depend on memory.
  • Kept the schedule organized and raised conflicts early.

The outcome

The founder had a more organized calendar and more reliable follow-through, and could stay focused on strategic priorities instead of managing logistics.
