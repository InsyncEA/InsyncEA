Business Operations Support

Structure for the parts of your business that currently run on habit and memory.

Problems this service solves

  • Work depends on one or two people remembering how it is done. When they are away or overloaded, things stall.
  • There are no written procedures. Every new task, and every new hire, starts from scratch.
  • Tasks live in inboxes, chats, and memory. Nobody can see in one place what is open, due, or stuck.
  • The same problems keep coming back. Nobody has the time to find and fix the process behind them.
  • Requests and handoffs have no clear owner. Work bounces between people and slows down.

What is included

Project coordination

Tasks, owners, and deadlines stay visible, so projects keep moving between meetings.

Task tracking

One place to see what is open, what is due, and what is stuck.

SOP creation and documentation

The way your business does things is written down, so it can be repeated, shared, and handed off.

Process improvement

Repeating problems are traced to the process behind them and fixed once.

Workflow organization

Requests, approvals, and handoffs follow a clear route instead of living in inboxes.

A real example: wholesale tea business operations

Orders arrived from several places at once: Shopify, wholesale portals, and email. I built one central tracker for orders, tracking numbers, due dates, and invoice status. The result was clearer visibility and far less manual follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Can you improve the processes we already have?

Yes. I start by mapping how work actually flows today, find the step where things slow down or get lost, and fix that first, instead of rebuilding everything.

Do you create SOPs from scratch?

Yes. I write them in plain language from how you already do the work, so your team can follow, repeat, and hand them off.

Do I need to change the tools I use?

Not necessarily. I work in the tools you already have where I can, and suggest a simple tracker if you do not have one.
