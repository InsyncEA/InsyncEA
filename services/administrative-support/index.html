What is administrative support?

Administrative support is the behind-the-scenes work that keeps a business’s information accurate, organized and ready to use: data entry, document preparation, reports, presentations and file organization. It is the work that is easy to postpone and expensive to get wrong.

It differs from executive assistant support, which manages one leader’s calendar and inbox, and from business operations support, which organizes how work flows. Administrative support is the hands-on preparation of the information itself.

Sound familiar?

If you or your team spend the first hour of the day on paperwork, you are not behind. You are doing work that should not need your skills.

  • Routine data entry and paperwork eat hours every week. The time comes straight out of work that needs your skills.
  • Documents are inconsistent or prepared at the last minute. Quality varies and deadlines get tight.
  • Reports and presentations take longer than the decisions they support. Pulling information together delays the real work.
  • Files and records are scattered. Finding one document means searching three places.
  • Small admin jobs keep interrupting higher-value work. Focus breaks, and the small jobs still pile up.

How handoffs work

  1. Share an example

    A template or a past report that shows what good looks like.

  2. Confirm format and deadline

    We agree the format, the deadline and who needs to see the result.

  3. Review the first piece

    A first piece is delivered for your feedback before anything is repeated.

  4. Repeat the same way

    The work follows the same standard each time, so you explain it once, not every week.

What your first 30 days look like

This is a typical pattern. The pace follows how much you want to hand over.

  • Week 1: examples, templates and formats collected, and the first task chosen.
  • Week 2: first deliveries made and adjusted from your feedback.
  • Week 3: repeat work runs to the agreed standard and schedule.
  • Week 4: a review of what is working, with more tasks handed over if you want them.

Common mistakes when handing off admin work

  • Handing over work without an example. Showing what good looks like saves rounds of corrections.
  • Leaving the deadline vague. A clear date and format keeps work predictable.
  • Skipping feedback on the first piece. Early feedback sets the standard for everything after it.
  • Having no file naming system. Consistent names and folders make records findable years later.
  • Sending the same data in several formats. One agreed format keeps entries consistent. Repeating tasks also work well as written procedures, as in how to create workflows for a small business.

Questions about administrative support

What kinds of documents can you prepare?

Letters, forms, templates, reports and presentations, formatted to your standards.

How do you keep data accurate?

I follow the format you set, check entries against the source and flag anything unclear instead of guessing.

Do I need to explain everything every time?

No. After the first handoff, your instructions and examples are saved, so repeat work follows the same standard.

Can you work with my existing templates and formats?

Yes. Share a template or a past example and the work follows it, so everything looks like it came from your business.

How do you protect sensitive documents?

You decide what can be accessed. An NDA and secure access tools, such as a password manager, are part of the setup.

What is the difference between administrative support and executive assistant support?

Administrative support handles the documents, data and reports. Executive assistant support is built around one leader’s calendar, inbox, meetings and travel.

How quickly can we start?

After the discovery call we agree on the first piece of work, the format and the deadline. Most clients start with one repeating task and add more.

How fast do you respond?

Same day, including weekends, in your time zone.
