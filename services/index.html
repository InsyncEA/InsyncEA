What are executive assistant services?

Executive assistant services are the day-to-day support a busy leader or team hands off: managing the calendar and inbox, organizing how work gets done, keeping customer and partner communication moving, and handling the documents and data behind it. Today much of it is delivered remotely by a virtual executive assistant.

InSyncEA groups that support into four areas, so you can start with the one that hurts most. Not sure what an executive assistant does? The FAQ covers the basics.

Sound familiar?

If you are the person everything runs through, you are probably living at least one of these.

  • Your calendar and inbox run your day. Real work happens in whatever time is left.
  • Things work, but only because you remember how. Nothing is written down, and nothing survives a busy week.
  • Customer and vendor messages are piling up. Replies slow down and follow-ups go cold.
  • Admin work keeps pulling you away. Small jobs interrupt the work that needs your skills.

One point of contact across all four areas

Each service applies the same approach to a different part of your business, and they work best together. Many clients begin with one and add a second once they see what a steady point of contact does for them. A common path is executive assistant support first, then business operations support once the calendar and inbox are under control.

What you can expect from every service

  • One clear point of contact. You always know who is handling what, and where to send a request.
  • Written priorities. We agree what matters most, so decisions about what to do first are made once, not daily.
  • Regular updates. Short, plain summaries of what is done, what is in progress and what needs your input.
  • Same-day replies. Including weekends, and in your time zone.
  • Access on your terms. You decide which accounts, calendars and folders can be used, and we agree the rules on the discovery call.
  • Systems that outlast the task. Where it helps, how something is done is documented, so anyone can repeat it.

Everything happens inside the tools you already use, including Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Asana, Trello, Notion, Calendly and Zoom, and new ones are picked up quickly.

Questions about executive assistant services

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Many businesses start with executive assistant support or a single operations problem and add more later.

What is the difference between executive assistant support and business operations support?

Executive assistant support protects a leader’s time: calendar, inbox, meetings and follow-ups. Business operations support improves how the work runs: task tracking, SOPs and workflows.

Do you only work with businesses of a certain size?

No. I work with founders, executives, small businesses and growing teams. What matters is having too many moving pieces, not a minimum headcount.

How do I know which service I need?

Use the table above, or book a discovery call. We talk through what is taking up your time and I recommend a starting point.

Can the services be combined?

Yes. Combining them gives you one steady point of contact across your calendar, your operations, your customer communication and your admin.

What do executive assistant services include?

Calendar and inbox management, task tracking and SOPs, customer and vendor communication, and data entry, documents and reports. Each has its own page with the detail.

How quickly can we start?

After the discovery call we agree on priorities and a start date. Most clients start with one area and add more as they get comfortable.

How fast do you respond, and do you work in my time zone?

Replies come the same day, including weekends. Most clients are on US Eastern Time, and working hours are adjusted to match yours.
