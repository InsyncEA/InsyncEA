Executive Support Services

Your calendar, inbox, and follow-ups, handled so your time goes to the work only you can do.

Problems this service solves

  • Your calendar fills up with the wrong meetings. Time for real work shrinks while requests you could have declined or delegated keep landing.
  • Important emails sit unanswered. Urgent messages get handled first, so the quieter but important ones wait too long.
  • Follow-ups slip after calls and meetings. Next steps are agreed in the room and forgotten by the end of the week.
  • Scheduling takes endless back-and-forth. Finding a time across several people can eat a surprising part of your day.
  • Travel and meeting prep land at the last minute. Itineraries, agendas, and materials get pulled together in a rush.

What is included

Calendar management and executive scheduling

Meetings are booked around your priorities, with conflicts and buffers handled before they become your problem.

Inbox management

Messages are sorted, replies are drafted or sent within the guidelines you set, and what needs your attention is surfaced.

Meeting coordination

Invitations, agendas, materials, and follow-up notes are handled, so meetings start prepared and end with next steps.

Travel coordination

Itineraries, bookings, and changes are organized in one place.

Follow-ups and reminders

Open loops are tracked until they close, so nothing waits on your memory.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work in my time zone?

I work remotely and agree overlap hours with you on the discovery call, so the windows that matter to you, such as morning calls, are covered. Much of the work, like inbox triage and follow-ups, does not need live overlap.

How do you manage confidential information?

We agree on access and confidentiality up front, on the discovery call. You decide what I can see and do, such as which inboxes, calendars, or folders, and I keep to it.

Can you send emails on my behalf?

Yes, within guidelines we set together. I can draft replies for your approval or send routine ones directly, and I flag anything that needs your decision.
