Frequently Asked Questions About Hiring a Remote Executive Assistant

Thinking about hiring a remote executive assistant? Here are answers to the questions founders and growing businesses ask most often, from tools and confidentiality to communication and getting started.

About the service

What is a remote executive assistant?

A remote executive assistant supports a leader or team from a distance, handling the calendar, inbox, meetings, follow-ups and day-to-day operations so you can focus on the work only you can do.

What is the difference between an executive assistant and an operations assistant?

An executive assistant mainly protects your time: calendar, inbox, meetings, and follow-ups. An operations assistant focuses on how the business runs: tracking, documentation, and processes. InSyncEA provides both, so you can start with one and add the other. Learn more: What Are Business Operations?

How is this different from a general virtual assistant?

A general virtual assistant typically completes the tasks you assign. An executive assistant takes ownership of follow-through and works to your priorities, and InSyncEA adds operations support such as workflows, SOPs and systems.

What services do you offer?

Four areas: executive assistant support, business operations support, customer service and partnership support and administrative support. Each has its own page, and you can see them together on the services page.

What industries do you work with?

Examples on this site include a nonprofit, an ecommerce store, a wholesale business and a WhatsApp-based AI product, plus founders and growing teams in general. See the case studies.

Is remote support right for a small business?

Often, yes. A small team feels every missed follow-up. Remote support gives you structure and capacity without hiring someone in-house.

Working together

Do I work with you directly?

Yes. You work with me directly, from the first call to the follow-ups that keep things moving.

How do we communicate?

Through the channel you already use, such as email, Slack or Zoom, with short, plain updates on where things stand. We agree how often during onboarding.

Can a remote executive assistant manage multiple executives?

Possibly. It depends on how many people and how much work is involved. We agree the scope on the discovery call, so support stays reliable for everyone.

Do you work in my time zone?

Yes. I adjust my working hours to match yours. Most clients are on US Eastern Time.

How fast do you respond?

Same day, including weekends.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

In most cases, yes. I can work inside your existing calendar, email, project management, and CRM tools, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Asana, Trello, Notion, Calendly and Zoom, and I pick up new ones quickly. We talk through access on the discovery call.

How do you handle confidential information?

We discuss access and confidentiality on the discovery call. You decide what I can see and do, and I keep to it. I am happy to sign an NDA and use secure access tools such as a password manager.

Getting started

Can I start with one service and add more later?

Yes. Many businesses begin with calendar and inbox support or one operations problem, then add more as they see what works.

What happens on the discovery call?

We talk through what is on your plate, what keeps slipping, and what you want off your list. Then we agree whether we are a good fit and what the first steps would be.

How is pricing structured?

Support is tailored to your needs, so pricing is agreed after the discovery call, once we know the scope.

What tasks should I delegate first?

Start with the work that is repetitive, time-consuming and does not need your judgment, such as calendar scheduling, inbox sorting and follow-ups. Once that runs smoothly, add the next area.

How quickly can we start?

After the discovery call we agree priorities, access and a start date. Most clients begin with one area and add more as they get comfortable.
